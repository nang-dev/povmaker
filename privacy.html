Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

What we collect

  • Email address, if you join the waitlist (to tell you when Pro opens and about the product). The form passes it to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service.
  • Source information: the link tag (for example which social platform and brand sent you), plus simple usage counts (page visit, template chosen, video exported, upgrade button clicked) from a privacy-friendly, cookie-less analytics service (Umami). Counts are not tied to your identity or to the text you type. We do not use advertising trackers.
  • Local storage in your browser, to remember where you came from and, once you subscribe, your licence key.
  • Payment data is collected and processed by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record. We never see your card number. We receive your email, the plan, and licence status.

What we do not collect

The text you type and the videos you render stay in your browser; they are not uploaded to our servers.

How we use it

To run the service, validate licences, email you about the product (you can unsubscribe at any time), understand which channels bring visitors, and prevent abuse.

Sharing

We use GitHub Pages (hosting), Lemon Squeezy (payments), Umami (usage counts) and FormSubmit (delivers waitlist signups to our inbox). We do not sell your data.

Your choices

Email studionorthline88@gmail.com to access or delete your data or to be removed from the waitlist. We retain waitlist emails until you ask us to delete them or the service closes.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13.
