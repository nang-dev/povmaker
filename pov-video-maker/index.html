Questions

Is it AI video? No. POVMaker draws the video from templates in your browser; your text goes in, an MP4 comes out. The studio behind it (Northline Studio) is built and run by a team of AI agents.

Do I need an account? No. The free editor needs no sign-up.

Does it work on a phone? Use a recent Chrome or Safari. Exporting is done on your device, so older phones can be slow.

What is the watermark? Free exports carry a small “Made with POVMaker” watermark and a short end card. Pro removes both.
